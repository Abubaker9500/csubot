# Campus services

## Parking

Official permit page: https://www.csub.edu/police/parking/permit.shtml

- Buy a semester parking permit at https://www.mycampuspermit.com
- Log in with myCSUB username and password
- You will need a credit card or e-check, vehicle license plate, and a mailing address
- After purchase, print the temporary permit and place it on the dashboard (good for 14 days until the permanent permit arrives)
- Student Union FAQ listed semester prices as $93 for cars and $22 for motorcycles; confirm current prices when purchasing
- Daily parking: $6 at ticket machines near campus street entrances (cash or credit)
- Parking Management Bureau: 1-800-700-4417
- Citation appeals: follow the instructions on the citation, or contact Campus Police at (661) 654-2111
