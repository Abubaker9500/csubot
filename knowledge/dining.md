# Campus dining

Official source: https://www.csub.edu/dining/locations.shtml
Meal plans: https://www.csub.edu/dining/meal-plans.shtml
Menus: https://csub.mydininghub.com/en/locations

Runner Cafe was rebranded. The main dining hall is now called Runner Market (formerly Runner Cafe).

Hours below are from the official dining locations page and can change. Confirm at https://www.csub.edu/dining/locations.shtml

## Runner Market (formerly Runner Cafe)

Hours: Monday–Sunday, 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Runner Market is the heart of campus dining, with three concepts under one roof:

- Italian Fare — baked pastas, sauces, rotating chef specials
- Global Tastes — melts, toasted sandwiches, paninis, wraps
- Mexican Grill — tacos, burritos, bowls, tortas, quesadillas

## Cafecito & Bakery

Hours: Monday–Friday, 7:30 AM – 6:30 PM

Coffee, espresso, breakfast, pastries, bakery items, grab-and-go meals, and snacks. Closed Saturday and Sunday according to the dining locations page.

## Local Tare & Taste

Hours: Monday–Friday, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM

Build-your-own, pay-by-weight meals with seasonal salads, grains, proteins, and vegetables. Located on the ground level of Science III.

## World in a Bowl

Hours: Monday–Friday, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM

Customizable bowls with grains, vegetables, proteins, and sauces. Located in the Student Union.

## Runner Express

Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Grab-and-go meals, sandwiches, salads, snacks, beverages, and convenience items.

## Hearty Continental / Residential Dining

Hours: Monday–Friday, 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM

Breakfast for residential students in the Community Center: warm breakfast items, fruit, cereal, yogurt, pastries, oatmeal, and beverages.

## Meal plans (2026–27)

Residential meal plans (semester prices from the official meal-plans page):

- White: 14 meals/week, $0 Dining Dollars, $2,720
- Blue: 15 meals/week, $200 Dining Dollars, $2,950
- Gold: 19 meals/week, $100 Dining Dollars, $3,175

Meal swipes can be used at Runner Market, World in a Bowl, Local Tare & Taste, Runner Express, Cafecito & Bakery, and Hearty Continental.

Meal cash equivalency (when used at participating retail): up to $8 breakfast, $11 lunch, $13 dinner.

Students must present a valid CSUB Campus ID.
