# Walter W. Stiern Library

Official hours: https://csub.libguides.com/library/hours and https://csub.libcal.com/hours/
Library home: https://www.csub.edu/library/
Hours change for holidays, finals, and summer. Always verify on the official hours page.

## Library hours (Fall 2026 regular schedule)

Typical hours during Fall 2026 (after summer session ended):

- Sunday: 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Monday–Thursday: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Saturday: 11:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Labor Day (Monday, September 7, 2026): library closed.
Veterans Day (Wednesday, November 11, 2026): library closed.
Thanksgiving: closed Thursday–Friday, November 26–27, 2026. On Wednesday, November 25 the library is listed as closed (closes at 5:00 PM on the hours calendar).

Extended hours near finals (late November into mid-December 2026): some weeknights extend to midnight (8:00 AM – 12:00 AM). Check LibCal for the exact finals-week dates.

Summer session hours (when in effect) have been Monday–Thursday 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM, closed Friday–Sunday. Fall 2026 is not on summer hours.

## 24-hour study room

The 24-hour study room and lockers are on the lower level of the Walter W. Stiern Library, near the lower entrance. A valid Runner ID is required for after-hours access. This room is typically open 24 hours even when the main library is closed, except on some holidays (for example Labor Day and Thanksgiving).

Group study rooms: https://csub.libcal.com/reserve
