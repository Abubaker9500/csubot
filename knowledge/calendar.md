## Fall 2026 semester

- All faculty due on campus: August 17, 2026
- First day of classes: August 24, 2026
- Last day to add classes: September 2, 2026
- Labor Day (campus closed): September 7, 2026
- Census Day / last day to withdraw without a W: September 21, 2026
- Veterans Day (campus closed): November 11, 2026
- Last day to withdraw for a serious and compelling reason: November 13, 2026
- No classes, campus open: November 25, 2026
- Thanksgiving (campus closed): November 26–27, 2026
- Deadline to apply for Spring 2027 graduation: November 30, 2026
- Last day of classes: December 7, 2026
- Study / reading day: December 8, 2026
- Examination (finals) period: December 9–15, 2026
- Commencement: December 12, 2026
- Grades due: December 18, 2026
- Winter break: December 19, 2026 – January 19, 2027
- Winter Session courses: December 21, 2026 – January 14, 2027
- Campus closed: December 25, 2026 – January 1, 2027

- First day of classes: January 25, 2027
- Last day to add classes: February 3, 2027

- Memorial Day (campus closed): May 31, 2027
