## Can I graduate with a D?

Yes — possibly, but it depends on which GPA and requirement the D affects.

Graduation requires a minimum overall GPA of 2.00 (C) and a minimum of 2.00 in the major, the minor (if applicable), and CSUB coursework. Only letter-graded courses (A, B, C, D, F, with plus/minus) are used in GPA computation. A D is a passing grade (1.0 points); it does not automatically block graduation.

Students cannot graduate with grades of I (Incomplete), RP (Report in Progress), or RD (Report Delayed) on their record. Those pending grades must be changed before the degree can be awarded.

Some specific requirements must be met with specified grades. GWAR by course requires a C- or better (or GWAR by exam with a score of 8 or higher). A D would not satisfy GWAR by course, or any other requirement that explicitly needs a C- or better.

Therefore:

- If the D keeps your overall GPA and required GPAs (major, minor, CSUB) at or above 2.00, you may still meet the GPA requirement for graduation.
- If the D causes any required GPA to fall below 2.00, you would need to raise that GPA (for example by repeating the course if allowed, or earning higher grades in other courses) to reach the 2.00 minimum.
- If the course with a D is being used to satisfy a requirement that explicitly requires a C- or better (for example, GWAR by course), a D would not satisfy that requirement.
- You cannot graduate with pending I, RP, or RD grades on your record.

If you want specific guidance for your record, consult your academic advisor or the Registrar to see how that D affects your major, minor, overall, and CSUB GPA and whether repeating the course or other actions are needed.

## GPA requirements

Graduation requires a minimum overall GPA of 2.00 and a minimum of 2.00 in the major, the minor (if applicable), and CSUB coursework. Only letter-graded courses (A, B, C, D, F, with plus/minus) count in GPA. D is passing (1.0 points).

## Incomplete and pending grades

Students cannot graduate with I (Incomplete), RP (Report in Progress), or RD (Report Delayed) on their record. Pending grades must be resolved before the degree is awarded.

## GWAR

The Graduation Writing Assessment Requirement (GWAR) by course requires a grade of C- or better. A D does not satisfy GWAR by course. GWAR may also be met by exam with a score of 8 or higher.
